# James Stavridis's career as a negotiator

*Status: VERIFIED (confirmed against sources)*

Admiral James Stavridis's career as a negotiator has been marked by his ability to navigate complex global challenges. As a 4-star officer in the U.S. Navy, he served as Supreme Allied Commander of the NATO Alliance from 2009 to 2013, with responsibility for operations in Afghanistan, Libya, the Balkans, Syria, counter piracy, and cyber security. This experience likely honed his negotiation skills, as he worked with international partners to address these complex issues.

After retiring from the Navy, Stavridis served as the 12th Dean of The Fletcher School of Law and Diplomacy at Tufts University, where he earned his PhD in international relations. He has since become a renowned author and public speaker, publishing 15 books and hundreds of articles on global security. His 2012 TED talk on global security has close to one million views, and he has been a Bloomberg opinion columnist and Chief International Security Analyst for NBC News.

As a negotiator, Stavridis has demonstrated his ability to navigate complex international relationships. As Supreme Allied Commander, he worked with NATO partners to address global challenges, and his experience in the Navy's premier operational think tank, Deep Blue, immediately after the 9/11 attacks, likely developed his skills in strategic planning and negotiation. His current roles as Partner and Vice Chair of Carlyle, a global investment firm, and Chair of the Board of Trustees of the Rockefeller Foundation, demonstrate his ability to navigate complex global challenges and negotiate on behalf of these organizations.
