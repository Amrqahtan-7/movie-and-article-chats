Netflix is an American video streaming service founded in 1997. The company is headquartered in Los Gatos, California, and is publicly traded on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol NFLX. Initially, Netflix disrupted the Blockbuster video-rental model by offering a per-rental fee for DVDs. The website was launched in 1998, allowing subscribers to select movies and TV shows and receive the discs by mail. In 2000, Netflix introduced a flat-fee unlimited rentals service with no due dates, late fees, or shipping fees.

In 2007, Netflix introduced streaming services that allowed subscribers to access content directly over the Internet. Streaming quickly gained momentum, becoming unlimited for most subscription plans. Netflix partnered with makers of video game consoles, Blu-ray players, and other electronics to enable video streaming on those devices. The company has since expanded its service to include original productions, many of which have been nominated for awards.

As of July 2026, Netflix received 111 Primetime Emmy Award nominations. The company has a strong presence in the entertainment industry, with over half a billion people in more than 190 countries and 50 languages being entertained by Netflix. The company's mission is to "entertain the world, one fan at a time," and it achieves this through its various services, including reach, recommendations, and fandom. Netflix's content is available in multiple languages and is programmed for just about every taste and culture.

In recent years, Netflix has been involved in various controversies, including criticism for its content and business practices. However, the company has also received praise for its innovative approach to the entertainment industry. In 2025, Netflix delisted 20+ games, including Hades and Monument Valley, from its platform. Despite this, the company continues to be a major player in the entertainment industry, with a strong focus on producing high-quality content that resonates with its subscribers.
