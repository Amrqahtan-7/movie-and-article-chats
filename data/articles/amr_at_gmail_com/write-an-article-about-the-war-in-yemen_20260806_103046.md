# write an article about the war in yemen

*Status: VERIFIED (confirmed against sources)*

The war in Yemen, which began in 2014, has become one of the world's worst humanitarian crises. The conflict started when Houthi insurgents, Shiite rebels with links to Iran, took control of Yemen's capital and largest city, Sanaa, demanding lower fuel prices and a new government. The Houthis later seized the presidential palace, leading President Abd Rabbu Mansour Hadi and his government to resign. In response, a coalition of Gulf states led by Saudi Arabia launched a campaign of airstrikes against the Houthis, which has continued to this day.

The Saudi-led coalition, which includes the United Arab Emirates, has carried out over 25,000 air strikes, causing over 19,000 civilian casualties. The Houthis have responded with a spate of drone attacks on Saudi Arabia and the UAE. The conflict has taken a heavy toll on Yemeni civilians, making Yemen the world's worst humanitarian crisis. The UN estimates that 60% of the estimated 377,000 deaths in Yemen between 2015 and 2022 were the result of indirect causes like food insecurity and lack of accessible health services. Two-thirds of the population, or 21.6 million Yemenis, remain in dire need of assistance, with five million at risk of famine and a cholera outbreak affecting over one million people.

The war has also led to a humanitarian blockade, imposed by the Saudi-led coalition, which has prevented aid from entering the country. The blockade has been at the center of the humanitarian crisis throughout the conflict. The United States, along with other NATO powers, has supported the Saudi-led intervention in Yemen primarily through arms sales and technical assistance. Human rights groups have criticized the countries for supplying arms, accusing them of being part of the Saudi-led coalition, which has imposed the blockade and blocked all ships from entering Yemen with supplies.

In recent years, the conflict has escalated, with the Houthis launching a series of unprecedented attacks against the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia. The Saudi-led coalition has responded with a bombing campaign in Sanaa, an attack on a northern prison, and a strike on a telecoms facility in Hodeida, resulting in a four-day internet blackout across the country. The conflict continues to this day, with no end in sight, and has become one of the most complex and volatile conflicts in the region.
